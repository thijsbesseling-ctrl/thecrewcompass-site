Support

Quickest is usually the app itself — most answers are in the FAQ.

In the app

Go to Profile → Feedback to report a bug or suggestion, with a screenshot if you like. It lands directly with the maker. Wrong or outdated content — a closed restaurant, a changed breakfast time — you can usually fix yourself with the edit button.

By email

support@thecrewcompass.app — for account questions, TestFlight trouble, privacy requests or anything that doesn't fit the app. You'll normally hear back within a few days (crew schedule permitting).

Delete my account & data

Email support@thecrewcompass.app from the address you signed up with and your account, favourites and personal data are removed. Places, photos and edits you contributed anonymously to the shared guide may remain, without any link to you.

The Crew Compass is an independent app made by crew. It is not affiliated with any airline.
