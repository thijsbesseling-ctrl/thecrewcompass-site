Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what crew ask most. Something else? Email support@thecrewcompass.app.

What happens to my roster when I link it?

Your roster never leaves your phone. The app reads your roster calendar (or uploaded roster) locally, on the device, and only looks for nightstops — which airport, which dates. Nothing about your flights, duties or crew composition is uploaded to our servers. Unlink it and the data is gone.

How do I get the app?

iPhone: the app runs in TestFlight, Apple's test platform. Install the TestFlight app, tap the invite link on this site, and The Crew Compass installs like any other app.
Android: download it directly via the button on the homepage — see below. You sign in with your email; an account is only used to sync your favourites and contributions.

How do I install it on Android?

The Android version comes straight from this site (not from the Play Store). Here's how:

  • Tap the Android button on the homepage — the file crewcompass.apk downloads.
  • Open the file from your notifications or Downloads.
  • Android asks once whether your browser may install apps — allow it and tap Install.

That warning is standard for any app outside the Play Store. New versions are announced by the app itself with an update banner.

Is it free?

Yes — the app is free for crew during the launch phase. At some point a paid version may follow; if that happens, you'll hear it in the app well in advance. No ads, and your data is never sold either way.

Can I add my own places and tips?

Please do — that's the whole idea. Paste a Google Maps link and the place fills itself in: distance from the crew hotel, opening hours, photos. You can also fix hotel details, add the arrival route at a station, or drop a photo of where the crew taxi waits. What you add goes live for the whole crew right away.

My airline isn't supported — now what?

Any crew member can already browse all destinations. But hotels, walking times and station routes are currently tuned to KLM and KLM Cityhopper. Use the form on the homepage to send us your roster PDF or roster calendar link — that's exactly what we need to add your airline, and you'll hear from us when it's ready.

Something is wrong or outdated in the app

Use Feedback in your profile inside the app (you can attach a screenshot), or simply correct it yourself — most content can be edited in place. For anything else: support@thecrewcompass.app.

The Crew Compass is an independent app made by crew. It is not affiliated with any airline.
